# Delete your Project Awesome Mobile account
Developer: Cool and Unusual Studio (Cool & Unusual) · App: Project Awesome Mobile

**In the Game:** Settings > Account > Delete account.
**Without the Game:** email lenn@coolunusualstudio.com from your account email, and include your Player ID or display name.

**What is deleted:** account, saves, Bucks and items, leaderboard entries, display name, PvP history. This happens within 30 days.
**What may be kept:** purchase records for tax purposes (generally up to 7 years), and ban/safety records (as long as needed to enforce a ban, keeping only the minimum needed: Player ID, ban reason and date, and hashed device identifiers).
Subscriptions (pre-launch: not yet available) must be cancelled in your app store account.

# Privacy Policy: Project Awesome Mobile

**Effective date:** October 23, 2026
**Last updated:** October 23, 2026

This policy explains how Cool and Unusual Studio ("we", "us"), doing business as Cool & Unusual, collects, uses and shares personal data when you play Project Awesome Mobile (the "Game") on iOS, Android or PC, or when you use our website.

**Contact:** lenn@coolunusualstudio.com · 1723 West 18th Street, Texarkana, TX 75501
**EU/UK representative:** if we are required to appoint one, we will list them here.

## 1. Adults only
The Game is for adults aged 18 and over. Before you can create an account, we ask for your date of birth. If you are under 18, you cannot create an account, and we do not keep the date of birth you entered. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. Contact us at lenn@coolunusualstudio.com if you believe a minor has an account.

## 2. Data we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |

|---|---|---|

| Account data | Player ID, sign-in method (e.g. Unity Player Accounts, Apple, Google, or anonymous/device sign-in), email address if you provide one, date of birth | You; sign-in provider |

| Profile and social data | Display name, avatar or house customisation, leaderboard scores, PvP match results, reports and blocks you make or receive | You; gameplay |

| Game data | Save data, progress, inventory, virtual currency ("Bucks") balances, earned loot box rewards | Gameplay (stored with Unity Cloud Save and Economy) |

| Device and technical data | Installation ID, device model, OS version, IP address, crash and diagnostic logs, IDFV | Your device; Unity SDKs |

| Advertising data (only if you choose to watch rewarded ads) | Advertising ID (IDFA/AAID) where permitted, ad interaction data, approximate location from IP | Ad partner SDK (not yet enabled; see section 4) |

| Purchase data (pre-launch: not yet available) | Transaction ID, item purchased, price, subscription status. We do **not** receive your full card number | Apple, Google, Steam |

| Support data | Messages you send us | You |

The first playable uses placeholder in-game money only. It processes no real payments.

## 3. How we use data, and our legal bases (EU/UK)
- **To provide the Game** (accounts, saves, cross-device sync, multiplayer, leaderboards): performance of our contract with you.
- **Age gate and safety** (keeping minors out, moderation, reports, bans, anti-cheat, server-checked scores): legal obligation and legitimate interests.
- **Rewarded ads** (only when you choose to watch): consent where required (e.g. personalised ads in the EU/UK, Apple ATT); otherwise legitimate interests.
- **Purchases and subscription (pre-launch: not yet available):** contract; legal obligation (tax and accounting records).
- **Analytics and crash reports** to fix bugs and improve the Game: legitimate interests, or consent where required. We will update this policy before adding any third-party analytics.
- **Support and legal requests:** legitimate interests and legal obligation.

We do not sell personal data for money. We do not use your data for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

## 4. Who we share data with
- **Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services):** Authentication, Cloud Save, Economy, Cloud Code, Lobby and Relay, and diagnostics. For most of these services, Unity processes data **on our behalf as our processor / service provider**, under Unity's Data Processing Addendum. **Unity Player Accounts** (if you sign in with a Unity account) is a service for which **Unity acts as an independent controller**, under Unity's own Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
- **Sign-in providers** (Apple, Google, Steam, as applicable) under their own privacy policies.
- **Ad partners:** rewarded ads are not yet enabled. We will update this policy, naming our ad partners and linking their privacy policies, before we enable ads. Ad partners may act as independent controllers.
- **App stores and payment processors (pre-launch: not yet available):** Apple, Google Play and Steam process payments as independent controllers.
- **Other players:** your display name, avatar, scores and PvP results are visible to other players.
- **Authorities and legal**, where required by law, or to protect rights and safety; and a successor in a merger or acquisition.

Under California law, ad-related sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising may count as "sharing". You can opt out (see section 7).

## 5. International transfers
Unity and our other partners may process data in the United States and other countries. Where EU/UK law requires it, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum, or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified.

## 6. Retention
- Account and game data: kept while your account is active, and erased within 30 days of an account deletion request, except as below.
- Technical logs (crash, diagnostic and server logs): kept about 90 days.
- Date of birth: used to confirm you are 18 or older. We keep only what is needed to show that this check was passed.
- Purchase records (pre-launch: not yet available): kept as long as tax law requires, generally up to 7 years.
- Ban and safety records: kept as long as needed to enforce a ban, keeping only the minimum needed: Player ID, ban reason and date, and hashed device identifiers to stop banned players from returning.
- Backups: overwritten within 30 days after erasure.

## 7. Your rights
Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA, the UK, and California and other US states), you may have the right to: access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a portable copy, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent at any time, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data or targeted advertising. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To use these rights, email lenn@coolunusualstudio.com or use the in-game settings. We may need to verify your identity. We answer within the time the law requires (generally one month under GDPR, or 45 days under CCPA).

**Ad choices:** rewarded ads are always optional. You can turn off personalised ads in the Game's settings, reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings, or decline Apple's tracking prompt.

## 8. Deleting your account
- **In the Game:** Settings > Account > Delete account.
- **On the web:** [our account deletion page](delete-account.md), for players who no longer have the Game installed.

Deletion removes your account, saves, Bucks balances, inventory, leaderboard entries, display name and PvP history. Unspent virtual currency and items are lost and are not refunded. Active subscriptions (pre-launch: not yet available) must be cancelled separately in your app store account. Some records may be kept as described in section 6.

## 9. Security
We use encrypted connections, access controls and our providers' security measures. No system is fully secure.

## 10. Changes
We will post updates here and change the "Last updated" date. If a change is material, we will notify you in the Game.
